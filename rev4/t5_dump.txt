P[Normal|None] [/B/C00000]Common errors to watch when marking
P[Normal|None] []“It is high time that the government promote …” → the past tense is needed (“promoted”).
P[Normal|None] []“make it a priority to + –ing” → base verb only (“to add”).
P[Normal|None] []“in collaboration with” used as a verb (“parents collaborate with schools should…”) → keep it as a phrase between the subject and the modal.
P[Normal|None] []Sentence boundaries: a participle phrase after a comma with no matching subject (“Living in a fast-paced society, academic pressure …”), or a new sentence after a comma (“… flexibility, nevertheless, they cannot …”) → full stop and a capital letter.
P[Normal|None] []“the elderly” treated as singular (“the elderlies”, “the elderly feels”).

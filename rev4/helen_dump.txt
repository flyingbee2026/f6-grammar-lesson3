P[Normal|None] []Although an extra period for examination preparation sounds attractive, cutting one of our two weekly PE lessons would do F.6 students more harm than good. + [/C00000]  [Topic sentence — states the stance]
P[Normal|None] []To keep us physically fit during the examination year, the school should make it a priority to protect both lessons and to improve what happens in them. + [/C00000]  [Suggestion 1 — today’s Pattern 2]

P[Normal|None] [/B/C00000]What to reward when marking
P[Normal|None] []Content: a clear stance on the cut itself, plus one realistic alternative — not just “PE is good for us”.
P[Normal|None] []Language: the target patterns used accurately; formal verbs (protect, invest in, combat) rather than “help”; no “I think it is good / bad”.
P[Normal|None] []Organisation: topic sentence first, then explain and exemplify, and a closing sentence that returns to the proposal.
P[Normal|None] [/B/C00000]Common errors to watch when marking
P[Normal|None] []“It is high time that the government promote …” → the past tense is needed (“promoted”).
P[Normal|None] []“make it a priority to + –ing” → base verb only (“to add”).
P[Normal|None] []“in collaboration with” used as a verb (“parents collaborate with schools should…”) → keep it as a phrase between the subject and the modal.
P[Normal|None] []Sentence boundaries: a participle phrase after a comma with no matching subject (“Living in a fast-paced society, academic pressure …”), or a new sentence after a comma (“… flexibility, nevertheless, they cannot …”) → full stop and a capital letter.
P[Normal|None] []“the elderly” treated as singular (“the elderlies”, “the elderly feels”).
